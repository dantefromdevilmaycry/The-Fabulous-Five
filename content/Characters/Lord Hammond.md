A lord from the southern reaches of [[Avalon]], Lord Hammond intersects paths with the party in the town of [[The Gilded Vale]], during [[The Lockdown]] implemented by [[Lord Raedric]].

Following [[the lockdown]], the party was anxious to secure passage out of the town, and learns that the only person in the town who could feasibly secure such passage for them, was lord Hammond. However, his help doesn't come readily - he enlists the party's help in locating his missing daughter, [[Medbeth]].

Claiming that they had taken pause in [[The Gilded Vale]] owing only to the sudden onset of her sickliness, Hammond tells the party that his squires had been busy locating a wyrm's egg to cure Medbeth's ailments, when she mysteriously vanished a week into their waylaid stay in The Gilded Vale.

Hammond was hiding the fact that he was forcing Medbeth into a marriage with [[Lord Raedric]] against her will, to secure political favor. Last the saw Hammond, they had placed him in possession of the now-possessed [[Medbeth]].
