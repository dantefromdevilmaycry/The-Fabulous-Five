[[Unnamed Devil]] is the result of whatever rite [[Tyrgil]] conducted on [[Medbeth]]; he claimed the rite was merely possession, which would make [[Unnamed Devil]] merely the Devil possessing Medbeth.

The party encounters this devil upon having taken up a bounty from [[Bleden Mark]], to hunt a demon haunting [[The Sewers under Copperlane]] and suspected to have been involved in [[Raedric's Murder]].

They enter battle with the devil, realizing at one point over the course of the battle that it had a cloaker attached to its head, at which point they restrain the devil, prompting the cloaker's attempted escape, which the party foils by striking it down, after it possesses [[Monarch Sugarbarr]] in an attempt to escape by swimming through the sewer canals.

The Devil makes it obvious that it is essentially new to life; having taken "birth" on the 59th of [[Lightcall]], when [[Tyrgil]] performed the rite. It takes a liking to [[Callisto]], perhaps perceiving some affinity in their nature with Callisto's identity as a Paladin, and [[Godtouched]] devoted to [[Rymrgand]].

It's surprised to learn there's a bounty on its head, and instructs the party to talk to [[The Disfavoured]] and convince them to keep out of the sewers. He beheads himself to give the party something to cash in the bounty with, and further requests that they get the priests of [[The Leaden Key]] further in the sewers to live it alone as well. 

At the end of [[Session 7]], the party once again encounters the Devil, upon following the tunnel out of [[Gareth]]'s cell into the sewer system under the city.
