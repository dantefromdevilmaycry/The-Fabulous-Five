Till the 86th year of the [[Age of Peace]], Elowen was a farmhand in one of the many plantations in Eastern [[Lithalen]]. By most accounts, it was on a particularly cold night in the middle of the [[Silvertide]] that year, that he was approached by his god, [[Tir-Cendelius]], and gained [[Enlightenment]].

Elowen gained conviction that civilization, culture and religion in its current form were deliberately clouding the true nature of reality from the people. He left his old life behind, and set out to travel Lithalen on foot - and over the next five years, amassed a massive following all over Lithalen, sparking a Civil War that lasted four years, and resulted in his forces usurping the [[Divine State]] of Lithalen.

Coming to power in the 95th year of the [[Age of Peace]], he revolutionized the regions under his control, establishing [[Tir-Cendelius]] as the state deity, and promoting an ethos of pursuing the light. He deposed of the nobility under his empire, and promoted extensive land reforms to break up the older power structures in Lithalen. His empire was genuinely prosperous and gained him innumerable devoted subjects across the lands.

His followers came to regard his as an incarnation of Tir-Cendelius himself, and at the peak of his influence, in the 118th year of the [[Age of Peace]], he declared Holy War on the Kingdom of [[Tamara]], and led a three-year long campaign aiming to "bring the people of [[Antrok]] into the light."

Over the course of his campaign, which came to be known as [[The Saint's War]], his forces did not lose a single battle, or ever yield ground - they were waging guerrilla warfare out of the mountains north of [[Mycarthia]] and east of [[Zenobia]], they laid siege to [[Valdadras]] and occupied huge tracts of land all along the coastline.

It was only when he led his forces into [[Mycarthia]] in the 121st year, that his campaign came to an end. Under guise of surrender, Mycarthia put up no visible defenses and invited Elowen to march into the city. He chose to approach the city alone, and triggered the [[Godhammer]], a bomb planted under the bridge at the city's southern entrance, crafted by twelve priests of [[Spiira]] explicitly for the purpose of killing a god-incarnate like Elowen. It was the 3rd of [[Flowercall]], Elowen was 236 years old.

After the Godhammer went off, and Elowen was killed beyond any shred of doubt, his campaign fell apart and most of his forces hastily retreated to Lithalen, where the vacuum left in Elowen's wake saw the slow reversal of his reforms and a return of the Divine State of Lithalen, with all its feudal and nobility-centric characteristics.

The Kingdom of [[Tamara]] has suffered greatly as a result of [[The Saint's War]] and all along the regions where these battles were fought, the strangest [[Soul Malady]] keep presenting themselves, notably the [[Voidborn Crisis]] plaguing the regions around [[Valdadras]].

The people of Tamara colloquially term these maladies Elowen's Legacy.
