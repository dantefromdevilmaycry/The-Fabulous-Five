The party investigated Medbeth's disappearance during [[The Lockdown]] in [[The Gilded Vale]] to gain favor with [[Lord Hammond]] and secure passage out of the town, but end up assisting [[Tyrgil]] in his plot to have her possessed and used as a tool to assassinate [[Lord Raedric]].

The party never encounters her in a lucid state, and hence has never talked to her or learnt of her life through a first person account.

In [[Session 6]], the [[Unnamed Devil]] claims that she was a bitter woman that resided among the nobility of [[Valdadras]] and held deep vitriol against a number of wicked people, that he now sought to extract revenge against, on her behalf.
