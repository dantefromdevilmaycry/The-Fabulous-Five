A herbalist by trade, Calisca met the party during their travels with [[The Caravan]]. Her mother has bid her journey north and east from [[Isadora]] to [[Zenobia]],  and tend to her aunt who has taken sick and been in dire need of a caretaker for several months. She has aspirations to start up a practice, and study Botany.

![[Calisca.jpg|180]]

While still with [[the Caravan]], she accompanied the party as they searched for a bush of Kelventari, to soothe [[Bachoven]]'s stomach. She witnessed the party encounter two wounded and disturbed [[Giant Elks]], and the reluctant battle that ensued. Barely surviving further adventures through the [[Bülwark]], and [[The Vlenfarthan hideout]] in the hills, she made her way to [[The Gilded Vale]] under the party's care, where she had a notable conversation with Winfrith, a Dwarven woman who ran the apothecary in the town, and whose mother had just been executed under Raedric's order. 

As the party left [[The Gilded Vale]] and charted course for [[Valdadras]], [[Calisca]] parted ways and set out to the ten-towns of [[Zenobia]].
