According to [[Baldric]], under his guidance, [[The Animancers]] had been treating and studying severe cases of [[Soul Maladies]] that [[Tunon]] had previously instructed them to beware of, and which [[The Sanitarium]] avoided accepting in any capacity.

Gareth was one such patient. He was admitted in the Sanitarium in the 4th week of [[Flowercall]], the very same year the party is investigating the matter. According to Baldric, he was suffering from an awakening - one of the more concerning [[Soul Maladies]], characterized by an individual remembering the lives his soul has lived previously, and being driven mad as a result. His condition seemed to be improving significantly, when in the 8th week of [[Lightcall]], it destabilized to the point that it became impossible to approach him without unleashing significantly power magical surges. He was stashed away within the facility as Baldric prepared to have him transported away with the help of an antimagic field.

Right before the attack on the Sanitarium, the party appealed to [[Jagged Remedy]] to have him cease his imminent assault on the Sanitarium. Shaking off their attempts to reason with him, [[Jagged Remedy]] instead goes into reverie about the importance of the young man, whom he did not acknowledge as Gareth. Jagged talked of [[The Leaden Key]] being an order centered around an immortal, prophetic leader that through the gift of awakenings, with each reincarnation remembered his past lives and contributions to the order. It was his conviction that Gareth was this same prophetic leader, returning to the order after an absence of 250 years.

When [[Kaelos]] attempted to approach Gareth's cell, he set off a powerful magical surge that severely wounded everyone in the vicinity, and the party implemented a hasty retreat out of the facilities. Later, the party approaches his cell as the sanitarium burnt, and finds it deserted, with the walls giving way to a tunnel... leading right into the sewer system underneath the city.
