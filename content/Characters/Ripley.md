Race: Human
Age: 21
Class: Wizard

Ripley is one of [[The Animancers]] working at [[The Sanitarium]] as a researcher and directly involved with patient care and treatment. She's skilled with magic, and fast on her feet.

She falls in the party's orbit when they first interact with [[Tunon]], intercept her communications with [[Ashen Forge]] and start unravelling the happenings at her workplace.

During the battle that breaks out outside the sanitorium over the course of [[The Animancy Protests]], she counterspells a fireball that would have left the entire building immediately up in flames, likely immolating everyone inside. She also provides healing to the flesh constructs that break past [[Baldric]]'s blade ward to enter the combat.

Last the party saw, at the end of [[Session 7]], she was coordinating the safe exit of people stuck in the higher levels of the burning Sanitorium.
