(Deceased)

Race: Human
Age: 31
Class: Rogue

Jagged Remedy was a key operative for [[The Leaden Key]], and was slain by the party during a battle over the course of [[The Animancy Protests]] in [[Valdadras]].

As a Rogue, his objective at the time of the protests seemed to have been to scout [[The Sanitorium]] and implement a plan of action to retrieve [[Gareth]] from within. In conversation with [[Nya Lotep]] before the protest breaks out, he also talks about the importance of producing evidence against [[The Animancers]] that would eventually get [[Animancy]] outlawed following [[The Trial]] imminently set to decide the fate of the science in Valdadras. He was convinced they would find things within the Sanitorium complex that once brought out and shown to the public, would lead them to "burn the damn place down."

[[Nya Lotep]] tips him off [[The Disfavoured]]'s plans to imbue pieces of armor with souls, and produce hardy constructs to fill their ranks. Jagged Remedy later confirms that acting on the tip, he scouted some Disfavoured facilities and saw the machinations for himself; finding them impressive.

The party attempts to get him to call of his plans to torch the sanitorium, finding his convictions unshakeable. Conversely, he regales the party with an esoteric tale of the immortal, prophetic Leader of the Leaden Key, which the order now suspected had returned to them after 250 years, through the awakened [[Gareth]].
