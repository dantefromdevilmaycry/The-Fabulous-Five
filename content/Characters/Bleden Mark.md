Race: Human
Age: 36
Class: Fighter

A captain of [[The Disfavoured]], stationed at [[Copperlane]] in [[Valdadras]]. Bleden Mark is tall, always seen with ruffled, pitch black hair and riddled with battle scars; one particularly deep one marking his face ear to ear.

Bleden Mark's primary interactions with the party have flowed through [[Nya Lotep]], following his immediate hostility to the party upon seeing a band of elves and [[Godtouched]] enter the city. He reveals his bias against followers of [[Tir-Cendelius]] and acquaints the party with the tenets of [[The Disfavoured]], and their devotion to [[Gor'Gorro]]. 

When the party brings up a bounty for the demon in [[The Sewers under Copperlane]] that they found at the Adventurer's Guild outside the City walls, they're able to negotiate for higher pay and are briefed on the situation by Bleden Mark. 

Afterwards, they turn in the [[Unnamed Devil]]'s head as proof of completion of the bounty and deceive Bleden Mark into thinking the sewers were still unsafe due to poisonous gas leaks.

The party had additionally been tasked with finding the people communing in an old headquarters that the priests of [[Woedica]] maintained under Copperlane, and getting them to leave, which they did not attempt to do.

[[Nya Lotep]] was able to secure work as a mercenary for [[The Disfavoured]], working as security during [[The Animancy Protests]] by appealing to Bleden Mark.
