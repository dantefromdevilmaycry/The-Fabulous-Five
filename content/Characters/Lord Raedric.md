Raedric was a young Elven lord that had recently acquired the deed to [[The Gilded Vale]] and its surrounding environs, following [[The Saint's War]] and the cheap sale of the deeds for these war-torn regions.

He comes into position as a lord right before the [[Voidborn Crisis]] takes the region like a plague. In an attempt to settle the lands amongst this trying predicament, he hires messengers to spread word that he would reward handsomely all settlers arriving to the lands around The Gilded Vale, and take up tilling the land or other productive trades.

He maintains a keep in the outskirts of [[Valdadras]], and a residence within the city, living dozens of miles away from the land he actually rules.

From the party's perspective, Raedric was persecuting people that he suspected to be disturbing elements to the natural order; directly blaming them for inculcating an inferior culture and a society that was inviting the Voidborn Crisis upon the population. The issue grew pressing for him as the birth of his firstborn child approached, resulting in him taking more and more drastic measures to punish behavior and practices he deemed troublesome.

The party reaches [[The Gilded Vale]] on the 58th of [[Lightcall]], 138 AP, and are greeted with the sight of the central plaza in the town having been turned into a public execution display. A dead Baobab tree served as the central piece in the display, with numerous bodies hanging from the tree and piled all around it. 

Interacting with people around the town, the party feels convicted to deal with Raedric, and achieve this end by colluding with [[Tyrgil]] and carrying out his plot that sees [[Medbeth]] possessed with a vengeful creature loyal to [[Javek]], turning into an assassin that would be delivered right to Raedric's doorstep.

Following this, the party learns that Raedric had indeed been murdered, upon reaching [[Valdadras]] on the 61st of [[Lightcall]]. The particular circumstances of [[Raedric's Murder]] remain uninvestigated.
