Race: Human
Age: 25
Class: Warlock

Tyrgil is a cultist devoted to [[Javek]] and active in [[The Gilded Vale]]. In [[Session 4]], the party collaborates with him to hatch his plan to have [[Medbeth]] possessed with a Devil loyal to Javek, a plan he came up with upon getting to know Medbeth over the course of her stay in his town.

He would meet her in the dead of night, as she sneaked out of the quarters she retained in the town inn to explore the town in disguise. He claims to have had a passionate affair with her over the course of a week, and consequently maintains that Medbeth is a woman devoted to vengeance and scorn, and the ideal devotee to Javek; he genuinely believes she would find purpose upon being made to serve the basal processes of revenge under Javek's guidance. He proposes to the party that upon completion, the possession rite would yield a creature fit to slay [[Lord Raedric]].

Whether Tyrgil truly aimed to plot [[Raedric's Murder]] or presented that paradigm as a sharp-tongued tactic to appeal to the party is unknown.
