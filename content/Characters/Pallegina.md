Race: [[Godtouched]] Human
Age: 29
Class: Paladin

Pallegina is a tall, [[Godtouched]] woman, working as a court diplomat for the City State of [[Isadora]], which lies at the southern edge of the continent of [[Antrok]], being the capital of THE [[Isadoran Republics]]. Her physiology is partially morphed with that of a bird's, owing to her being touched by [[Hylia]], Patron Goddess of the Birds, Nature and Harvest.

![[Pallegina.webp|180]]

She met the party during their travels with [[The Caravan]]: The party ends up negotiating with [[The Vlenfarthans]] to have them spare Pallegina after their ambush. Her abilities as a Paladin prove to be of little help as the party navigates through [[The Vlenfarthan hideout]], due to her severe injuries.

Once they made it out, she accompanied the party to [[The Gilded Vale]], seeking audience with [[Lord Raedric]]. However, upon observing the situation there, resultant of Raedric's madness, she swiftly drops those plans. The last the party saw of her, she was headed to [[Mycarthia]] in order to begin an important assignment. The party parted ways with her as they charted course to the Port city of [[Valdadras]].
