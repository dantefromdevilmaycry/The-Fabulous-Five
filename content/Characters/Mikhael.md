Mikhael is a animancer initiate, performing research under Baldric at [[The Sanitorium]] in [[Valdadras]].

[[Bachoven]] encounters Mikhael in the basement of [[The Sanitorium]], in a state of acute distress regarding his stressful work environment and the ever-mounting stakes of practicing [[Animancy]] in Valdadras. He's overworked, and hasn't left the facilities in over a week. He's losing his passion for Animancy and his determination to make something out of himself.

Bachoven offers him some rum out of his [[Decanter of Endless Booze]], and sincere companionship. Mikhail mentions that he frequents a park in [[Ondra's Grift]], behind Harry's bookshop, and offers his name. 

Later as the sanitorium catches fire, an unconscious Mikhail is rescued out of the building by the party.
