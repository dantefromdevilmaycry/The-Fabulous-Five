A widely miscellaneous district within [[Valdadras]], built mostly out of old cobblestone and sheer marble, with a canal flowing through the middle of the district, bisecting it. - Inns, a large Amphitheatre, various City Governance buildings, a small marketplace, domiciles for various acolytes and scriveners, as well as housing for some of the city's nobility make up some of the prominent buildings within this small district situated as one of the two districts leading to a city gate. 

Copperlane houses the Library of Revealed Mysteries, a church to [[Zael]] being headed by [[Father Harold]].
