The largest district within [[Valdadras]], housing most of the city's ports, dry docks, warehouses, and guilds. Most buildings here are made out of hardwood or stone - floorspace is generally cramped, and the district houses many workers that practice trades out of the city. 

[[The Sanitarium]] is situated within Ondra's Grift, in posher section of the district bordering [[Heritage Hills]]
