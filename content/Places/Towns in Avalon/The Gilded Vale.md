#place
The Gilded Vale is a small town in central [[Antrok]], under the lordship of [[Lord Raedric]]. It frequently sees travelers keen for some rest on the long road connecting [[Isadora]] and [[Mycarthia]], and as such, has an unusually large and bustling inn, relative to the size of the town itself. Most people within the town practice quaint trades they can benefit out of offering to passing travelers - the town boasts of an armory, a tannery, an apothecary, and a bustling marketplace. Central to the town's old identity are the ruins of a pre-war temple of [[Spiira]] that [[Elowen]] is said to have destroyed over the course of his campaign in Antrok.

![[The Gilded Vale.jpg]]

Seventeen years ago, [[The Saint's War]] completely razed this region, allowing Raedric to acquire the deed to these lands for a paltry sum. Since then, he has been attempting to revive this erstwhile ghost town by offering people lucrative sums of money to settle down in The Gilded Vale and till the land, or perhaps practice a trade. This is precisely the sort of people that [[The Caravan]] was commissioned to bring into the Gilded Vale.
