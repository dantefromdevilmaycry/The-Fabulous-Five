
Only [[Kaelos]] and [[Callisto]] were active for this session, which saw them discover a plot laid out by a couple townsmen to orchestrate [[Raedric's Murder]], using [[Medbeth]] as a host for a scornful creature loyal to [[Javek]]. 

The party investigates the town and discovers a network of tunnels under the town, connecting the ruins of the old Spiiran temple to various other facilities through secret passageways. Following the passageways, and figuring out the secret to navigating under the city, they get the drop on [[Tyrgil]] and his assistant performing a ritual on an incapacitated Medbeth, in a portion of the passages directly under the tannery. 

The party listens to [[Tyrgil]]'s justification - He's devoted to [[Javek]], and has been lying in wait to act against [[Lord Raedric]] for months. He convinces the party that there's no straightforward way to save Medbeth at this point in the Ritual; as a bride promised to Raedric, she held immense scorn for him - she'd make for an ideal vessel to channel a creature subservient to Javek. He proposed that he get to finish the ritual, and the party return the now possessed Medbeth to [[Lord Hammond]], and collect their reward. They believed that once possessed, Medbeth would be able to slaughter Raedric with ease; Hammond was going to escort her straight to Raedric, who would be looking for a new bride in the wake of his child being [[Voidborn]]... It was taken for granted that this situation would have prompted him to have his wife executed.

The party went along with his plan, brought the possessed Medbeth, now lucid and eager to slaughter Raedric, straight to Hammond in the room he kept at the inn. Negotiating pay and passage out of the town, the party bid goodbye to Winfrith and left The Gilded Vale well before the crack of dawn, under [[Lord Hammond]]'s protection. Once safely away from town limits, [[Pallegina]] and [[Calisca]] bid them goodbye, and took the route North, to [[Mycarthia]] and [[Zenobia]].
