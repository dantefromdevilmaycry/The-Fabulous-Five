[[Callisto]], [[Bachoven]] and [[Kaelos]] arrive seek shelter in [[The Vlenfarthan hideout]] as a [[Bülwark]] wrecks havoc outside, with [[Calisca]] and an injured [[Pallegina]] in tow.

The pressing concern for them as they entered was to shelter against the [[Bülwark]] in this open cave system; find a section within where they could tend to their wounds without the magical winds reaching them, but the further they went in, the more traces of [[The Vlenfarthans]] they found - this cave system was one of their hideouts.

While exploring, they came across a chest next to a dead Dwarf, presumably one of [[the Vlenfarthans]]. Within the chest, they found a couple items - [[Kaelos]] claimed a [[Mysterious Dagger]], and [[Callisto]] took possession of a pair of Dimensional shackles. On the Dwarf's person, they found a note written by their lover; speaking of the importance of the task they had at hand, wishing them safety and much love.

Navigating the various passages, they happened upon a trapped tome, and chose to take an hour deciphering it, electing not to remove it from where it lay, ominously. It was written in Sylvan; consisting of an account of one fairy by the name of Rowan, who spent many years living amongst [[the Vlenfarthans]] and held much respect for their way of life. The account largely spoke of their determination to guard the knowledge of the Ancient Ones, which they believed the civilizations of now had strayed away from. It spoke glowingly of a old philosophy that did not place any weight in Divinity and the gods, and largely saw them as despots. It went on to theorize that [[Elowen]]'s desire to spread the light might have been a sincere attempt to convince the world that the gods weren't Divine. The speculation was plagued with self-doubt, unable to provide sound reasoning for [[Elowen]]'s devotion to [[Tir-Cendelius]]. Nevertheless, it sought to venerate [[Elowen]] as a messiah that would have brought [[Antrok]] and its people closer to an ancient truth they've now long forgotten.

The party found a hidden corridor, doused in magical darkness, which they saw fit as a location for a long rest. heading deeper into the dungeon, they faced a narrow stairway down through the passageway where they were attacked by a particularly vicious patch of green slime, which was steadily eating through their armor and equipment and dealing significant damage on contact. [[Kaelos]] attacked it with their newly acquired Dagger, as the slime clung to Calisto. upon using it for the first time, they were able to figure out that the Dagger was cursed; each day, the first time it touches living flesh, it deals a large amount of poison damage to the creature it comes in contact with. It seems to have a mind of its own, and seeks to come into contact with any flesh that it can.

The party encounters a large group of Vlenfarthans near another exit leading out of the cave system, and promptly dispatch them. Upon exiting the caves, they are able to confirm their suspicion that the [[Bülwark]] has passed, and happen upon a strange sight. They had walked into the heart of some ancient ruins, weathered beyond investigation to their faculties. At the center of the scene stood a tall drum, a pillar that looked as if its various segments could rotate once, before it had fossilized into stone. Around the drum were multiple robed figures, turned to stone in silent prayer.

After taking in the strange scene, the party set forth on a two-day journey to [[The Gilded Vale]], on foot.
