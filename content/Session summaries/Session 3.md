[[Bachoven]], [[Kaelos]], and [[Callisto]] were active for this session, which started out with their arrival in [[The Gilded Vale]] in the dead of night and ended with them beginning to put some pieces together while investigating Medbeth's disappearance. 

Upon their arrival, the party discovers dozens of corpses strung from a giant dead Sycamore tree overlooking the town plaza. Through conversation with Raedric's townguard, they are able to surmise that Raedric is on an inquisition. The [[Voidborn Crisis]] has hit his lands in a particularly bad fashion; over the past half-year, no child has been born possessive of a soul. With his firtborn's birth due within the week, he had grown overzealous in an attempt to swiftly eradicate all perceived causal elements to the crisis. Apparently, he has reason to believe that the crisis, far from being just [[Elowen]]'s legacy as the popular consensus among his subjects remains, is plainly the result of a moral and ethical failure of the people.

The party is instructed to spend the night at the inn; they would be given the opportunity to meet with Raedric the following day. However, [[Kaelos]] and [[Callisto]] land themselves in trouble with the manager of the inn, and are barred from taking up boarding within. Under such circumstances, they roam the town at night and interact with various townspeople to get a pulse of the situation at hand; ultimately fashioning beds for themselves in the wilderness surrounding the town, only a few hundred feet behind the inn itself. 

The following morning, the town gathers around the plaza after the townsguard rings out a pair of bells in dissonance with one another; the town was to be placed under Lockdown - effective immediately. [[The Lockdown]] was widely speculated to be a response to Raederic's child being [[Voidborn]]. If so, nobody would be permitted to leave till his conscience was satisfied.

Shortly after, a Dwarven woman that the party had interacted with the night before, owner of the apothecary, was executed; her body added to the growing crowd dangling from the Sycamore tree. The party eventually seeks out the Apothecary, now manned by the old woman's daughter, Winfrith. [[Calisca]] particularly takes an interest in Winfrith, owing to her background as a herbalist.

Eventually the party finds a lead; a powerful noble, [[Lord Hammond]], could easily secure exit from the town for himself and his party. It's also no secret to the town that the man has been deeply desperate for a couple days - A young woman he had been travelling with, supposedly his daughter, had gone missing. He wasn't leaving without her. 

A conversation with Hammond revealed that they had made a stop at The Gilded Vale solely to tend to his daughter [[Medbeth]]'s sudden onset of a strange illness. They weren't far from their destination and had elected to not leave until she was in better shape. They had spent over a couple weeks in town, by the time of Medbeth's untimely disappearance.

Enquiries around town yielded multiple divergent trails to investigate, rumors to ponder. Some suspected Hammond wasn't the girl's father but her uncle; intent on marrying her himself, prompting her daring escape. Others suspected plots wilder, still.

Ultimately, the party contended back and forth with Winfrith, and [[Tyrgil]], the owner of a tannery near the outskirts of town. Winfrith revealed that [[Medbeth]] was pregnant, and deeply unhappy. She had seen her sneak away to meet with [[Tyrgil]] on multiple occasions, and suspected that he might have hatched a plot to either deceive her into capture, or facilitate her escape. [[Tyrgil]] on the other hand maintained that he had been having an affair with Medbeth, and that she was faking her illness to stall for time to escape. He flatly believes that she managed to escape unaided by anyone from the town; possibly through collusion with one of her father's men.
