The second month of the year is Lightcall; the sun shines bright upon the lands, the seas are as still as they get, and the natural world is harmonious and kind. This is the season of adventuring, and travel. Weather is not observed to fit defined patterns this time of the year — it varies dramatically week to week, and harmless, fantastical weather phenomenon present themselves readily.

It is 70 days long, same as the other two Seasons; comprising of ten weeks.
