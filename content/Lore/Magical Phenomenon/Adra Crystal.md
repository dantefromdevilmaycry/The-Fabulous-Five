Adra is a rare material found throughout [[The Great Seas]].

An organic material crystalline in structure but shell-like in substance, Adra can be found growing up through the ground in veins in many parts of The Great Seas in massive pillars. Adra pillars are believed to be interconnected into a single giant network that reaches deep beneath the surface of Eora, but this has never been conclusively proven due to the lack of necessary technology. Adra is particularly abundant in [[Lithalen]] and the [[The Wastelands]], both of which were home to a long-lost civilization.

Adra is a magically potent substance, characteristically tied to life energy. The material acts as a conduit for souls, both capable of storing and transporting them over vast distances. Over the natural process of life, death and rebirth - souls travel along Adra as if it were a road leading beyond our realm. With the right technology, Adra can be used to manipulate souls, from transferring them between living beings, into animats, and constructs - all the way to redirecting, binding, and even destroying them on a vast scale.

[[Animancy]] makes extensive use of Adra Crystals to power its machinations.

[[The Disfavoured]] and [[The Animancers]] in [[Valdadras]] were seen using Adra Crystals in such endeavors as described above.
