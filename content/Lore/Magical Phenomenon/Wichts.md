At the beginning of the [[Voidborn Crisis]], untold number of children were born without a soul which resulted in their death in infancy. People sought the help of Animancers who came up with the solution of inserting animal souls in the children, as using lost human souls resulted in that soul overtaking the body. By inserting animal souls, the children gained awareness and volition, and [[The Animancers]] believed that given time they would develop into 'normal' people because the animal souls have less personality and memory. The children lived past infancy and developed some faint personality, but their interactions with others were stunted and 'weird'. When the children started reaching adolescence they began exhibiting physical transformations that turned them into Wichts. These Wichts would then seek to leave human civilization and make for the wilderness.

[[Monarch Sugarbarr]]'s druidic grove interacted with such Wichts, living within their own communities in the woods east of [[Zenobia]]

The party suspects that the push for using treatments transplanting animal souls might have come from [[Baldric]], following [[Tunon]]'s death; maybe even that Baldric orchestrated Tunon's murder without Tunon realising it.
