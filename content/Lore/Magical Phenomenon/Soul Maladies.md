Illnesses that negatively effect the essence of one's being, often leading to madness or death.

[[Animancy]] as a science is concerned with conceptualizing and treating these maladies, such as [[Awakening]]s, fracturings, and of recent note, the [[Voidborn Crisis]] around [[Valdadras]].

Pubescent children transforming into [[Wichts]] has also been classified as a Soul Malady by some Animancers, but remains a phenomenon notably distinct from other observed maladies.
