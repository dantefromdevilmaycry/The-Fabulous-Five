Desperate to end the war and eliminate [[Elowen]], [[Spiira]]'s clergy in [[Tamara]] developed a special bomb that was hidden beneath the Evon Dewer bridge at [[Mycarthia]]. The Godhammer Bomb combined both the magical prowess of twelve of Spiira's finest priests, and the goddess' own power with conventional high explosives created with the aid of Petrenae's finest artillery specialists. The bomb was fitted with a magical detonator consisting of an [[Adra Crystal]] infused with a fragment of [[Elowen]]'s soul, suspended on copper spikes. This fuse would only detonate if Elowen approached it, guaranteeing an explosion. An Illusion spell was cast over the bomb to conceal it.

Its detonation killed Elowen.
