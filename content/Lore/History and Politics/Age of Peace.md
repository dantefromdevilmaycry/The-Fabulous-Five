The campaign started out in the 138th year of the Age of Peace

This calendar system marks an era of relative harmony around [[The Great Seas]]. The Seven [[Divine State]]s came together to swear not to wage war with each other 138 years ago—the only infraction this oath has seen in all these years was 17 years ago, when [[Elowen]] led the forces of [[Lithalen]] into [[Antrok]], invading its northern kingdom of [[Tamara]].
