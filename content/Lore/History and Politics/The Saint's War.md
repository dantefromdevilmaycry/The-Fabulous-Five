The Saint's War was a campaign fought over three years, with [[Lithalen]]'s forces engaging the Kingdom of [[Tamara]], in what ultimately came down to a battle of attrition. Under [[Elowen]]'s command, the imperative of the invading Lithanii forces was to  "bring the people of [[Antrok]] into the light."

Over the three years from the 118th, to 120th year of the [[Age of Peace]] , the Lithanii forces did not lose a single battle, or ever yield ground - they were waging guerrilla warfare out of the mountains north of [[Mycarthia]] and east of [[Zenobia]], they laid siege to [[Valdadras]] and occupied huge tracts of land all along the coastline - effectively cutting off most of Tamara's population centers from productive farmland. 

Tamara suffered devastating loses with each battle, having a significantly smaller military force. Most of the plains that made up central Tamara were effectively under Elowen's control by the 120th year of the [[Age of Peace]].

It was only when he led his forces into [[Mycarthia]] in the 121st year, that his campaign came to an end. Under guise of surrender, Mycarthian forces put up no visible defenses and invited Elowen to march into the city. He chose to approach the city alone, and triggered the [[Godhammer]], a bomb planted under the bridge at the city's southern entrance, crafted by twelve priests of [[Spiira]] explicitly for the purpose of killing a god-incarnate like Elowen. It was the 3rd of [[Flowercall]], Elowen was 236 years old.

After the Godhammer went off, and Elowen was killed beyond any shred of doubt, his campaign fell apart and most of his forces hastily retreated to Lithalen, where the vacuum left in Elowen's wake saw the slow reversal of his reforms and a return of the Divine State of Lithalen, with all its feudal and nobility-centric characteristics.

The Kingdom of [[Tamara]] has suffered greatly as a result of [[The Saint's War]] and all along the regions where these battles were fought, the strangest [[Soul Maladies]] keep presenting themselves, notably the [[Voidborn Crisis]] plaguing the regions around [[Valdadras]].

The people of Tamara colloquially term these maladies Elowen's Legacy.
