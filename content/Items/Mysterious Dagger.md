
[[Kaelos]] found this magical Dagger in [[The Vlenfarthan hideout]], in the possession of a Dead Dwarf, also in possession of a pair of dimensional shackles and seemingly on an important mission. No clear reason for this person's death was identified, just that their death had taken them by surprise.

The Dagger was found to do massive poison damage to the first creature whose flesh it touched, every single day. The knife has been used in this manner twice, now.

- The first, against a green slime within the hideout itself, the day after the dagger was found - dealing 2d10 poison damage.
- The second, seven days later, against [[Jagged Remedy]], at the battle that broke out during [[The Animancy Protests]] outside [[The Sanitorium]] - dealing 7d10 poison damage.

[[Kaelos]] has a feeling that the Dagger is cursed, and has been reserving use of the Dagger remarkably so far, keeping it tightly wound when he carries it on his person.
