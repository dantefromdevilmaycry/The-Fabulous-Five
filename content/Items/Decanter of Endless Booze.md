Once per day, after dawn, speak the name of an alcoholic beverage and the decanter will present it in endless supply for the duration of the day.

Roll 1d4 to determine the quality of the beverage produced over the day:
- 1 - Takes like piss
- 2 - Poor Quality
- 3 - Standard Quality
- 4 - The finest booze in the lands

You can use an action to remove the stopper and speak one of three command words:

- "Stream" produces 1 gallons of the beverage.
- "Fountain" produces 5 gallons of the beverage.
- "Geyser" produces 30 gallons of the beverage that gushes forth in a geyser 30 feet long and 1 foot wide. As a bonus action while holding the decanter, you can aim the geyser at a creature you can see within 30 feet of you. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or take 1d4 bludgeoning damage and fall prone. Instead of a creature, you can target an object that isn't being worn or carried and that weighs no more than 200 pounds. The object is either knocked over or pushed up to 15 feet away from you.



Discovered inside a sarcophagus left ajar in the city crypts, and acquired by [[Bachoven]] while investigating the disappearance of Mikhael, a scrivener for the Public Archive of Secrets, an institute affiliate to the church of [[Zael]]; clearly the property of a group of scriveners given to excessive alcohol consumption, and defacement of the tombs of city nobility.
