#event
Upon Reaching [[Valdadras]] on the evening of the 61st day of [[Lightcall]], in [[Session 5]], the party learns that [[Lord Raedric]] is dead. He held a residence within Valdadras, in the [[Heritage Hills]] district, and was present in the city, anticipating the birth of his child.

[[The Disfavoured]] guards that the party came across maintained that a demon is responsible for the slaughter, and that it had escaped underground after massacring Raedric's entire residence; offering a bounty to the party for hunting it down.

The party later comes across an [[Unnamed Devil]] in [[The Sewers under Copperlane]], who recognizes [[Kaelos]] and [[Callisto]] as being responsible for its birth.

They had earlier, during their brief stay in [[The Gilded Vale]], assisted [[Tyrgil]] - follower of [[Javek]] - with his plan of having a vengeful entity loyal to [[Javek]] possess Medbeth. With the explicit hope that this creature would then be presented to [[Lord Raedric]] as Medbeth was meant to be, and slaughter him on sight.

The party does not suspect thing played out much differently, as corroborated by the [[Unnamed Devil]] in brief conversation.
