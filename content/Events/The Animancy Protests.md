#event
Popular perception of [[Animancy]] has plummeted over recent years in [[Avalon]], in the wake of the [[Voidborn Crisis]] and the transformation of several [[Voidborn]] treated by Animancers into [[Wichts]] upon approaching Maturity.

Throughout the later half of [[Lightcall]], 138 AP, popular protests have been stirring in [[Valdadras]], dissenting against the heavy presence of Animancers within the city; fearing their presence would bring [[Elowen]]'s Legacy more harshly upon the city, and generally being disillusioned with their practices.

During [[Session 7]], at dusk on the 83rd of [[Lightcall]], the protests centered around [[The Sanitarium]], one of the more significant facilities of [[The Animancers]] and erupted into violence.

[[Nya Lotep]], [[Kaelos]], [[Bachoven]], and [[Callisto]] intimately understand that [[Jagged Remedy]], and [[The Leaden Key]] at large are directly responsible for inciting violence at the protest in this instance.

The present situation, as of [[Session 7]] sees the Sanitorium having taken extensive structural damage from Alchemist fire, and still burning.
