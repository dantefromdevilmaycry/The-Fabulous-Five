#event 
On the 59th of [[Lightcall]], 138 AP, the day after their arrival at [[The Gilded Vale]], the town is put under lockdown following an unknown disaster surrounding the birth of [[Lord Raedric]]'s firstborn child. Speculation around the town, following the most plausible scenario, claimed that the child must have been [[Voidborn]].

Following the placement of the lockdown, several people suspected to be enemies of the natural order are put to death by the town guard, and strung up from the dead tree occupying the central plaza. The lockdown's purpose is revealed to be finding and eliminating the forces spreading the [[Voidborn Crisis]] in Raedric's lands.

Over [[Session 4]], the party decides to secure [[Lord Hammond]]'s favour to secure passage out of the town, and in the process of looking for [[Medbeth]] on his behest, become accessories to a plot hatched by [[Tyrgil]], a local follower of [[Javek]], to possess the vengeful Medbeth with a creature capable of slaughtering Raedric when Medbeth would be produced before him.

Ultimately, [[Kaelos]] and [[Callisto]] produce the possessed Medbeth to the oblivious [[Lord Hammond]], and having secured his favor, ride out of [[The Gilded Vale]] in the dead of night, with the Lockdown still in place.
