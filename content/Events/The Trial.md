#event 
The party has learnt of a trial to be held in [[Valdadras]], concerning [[Animancy]] - in face of public disapproval and [[The Animancy Protests]], the Court seeks to either vindicate or condemn the practice of Animancy around Valdadras.

The party has observed [[Baldric]] be excessively stressed about the approaching Trial, and concerned about the plummeting public image of [[The Animancers]] in the face of the protests.
