
The Vlenfarthans are a tribal people that live across [[Antrok]], centering their settlements around the ruins of a much older civilization. They are known to be extremely defensive of these ruins, and usually do not tolerate other people's attempts at exploring or pillaging these ruins.

The party's first interaction with the Vlenfarthans was when a group of these tribesmen ambushed [[The Caravan]]; after it had set up camp for the night in a quiet hillside clearing. They won this initial battle, leaving [[Pallegina]] as the sole survivor. In a conversation with the party that secured [[Pallegina]]'s release; they blame [[the Caravan]] for tampering with some old ruins in the vicinity, and for causing the imminent [[Bülwark]] storm. 

This encounter ended as the [[Bülwark]]'s edge came into sight and the raiding party scampered towards the hills. The party eventually followed, and found their way into [[The Vlenfarthan hideout]], a cave system tucked within a narrow valley.
